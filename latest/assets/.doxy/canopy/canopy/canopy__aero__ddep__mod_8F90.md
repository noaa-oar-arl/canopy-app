## Detailed Description


Implements sub-canopy aerosol dry deposition following Katul et al. (2010) [https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2009JD012853](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2009JD012853) and issue #148. Calculates deposition velocities for aerosols in multilayer canopy.
